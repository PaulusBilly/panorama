# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People with a Stremio account and their own installed addons who want to sit down and watch a film on a Mac (Apple Silicon) or Windows PC. Usually evening, lean-back, often a dim room. The jobs are picking up a film they were already watching, finding something to watch, and getting it playing with good audio and subtitles.

## Product Purpose

An unofficial native Stremio client for films. It turns the user's addons into a place to browse, search and watch, with native mpv playback. Success is opening the app and being one click from the film you were watching, and the app feeling like a cinema rather than a file browser.

## Brand Personality

Cinematic, confident, quiet. The film's artwork is the hero and the interface steps back. Bold uppercase titles and one decisive cobalt accent give it identity; everything else stays out of the way. Reference: the Billy-supplied streaming home screen with an icon sidebar, full-window backdrop, pill actions and a Continue row.

## Anti-references

- Generic streaming-app slop: purple gradients, glowing cards, rounded-everything tiles, carousels of identical cards with badges.
- File-browser or admin-panel feel: dense tables, chrome competing with artwork.
- Decorative glassmorphism; translucency only where it keeps buttons legible over artwork.

## Design Principles

1. Artwork first. Interface chrome never competes with the film still.
2. One click to resume. What you were watching is the first thing you see.
3. One identity everywhere. Every screen shares the same dark canvas, type and button language.
4. Never look broken. Missing artwork, signed-out or offline states keep the layout and stay calm.
5. Keyboard is a first-class input. Every control is focusable with a visible ring.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Text over artwork must stay legible on any still (scrims hold contrast). Full keyboard navigation with visible focus. Reduced-motion flag (`--reduced-motion`) disables transitions. Screen-reader labels on icon-only controls.
